Poker without the casino noise.

Felt is a focused, single-player Texas Hold’em table built for quick games and better instincts. There are no accounts, ads, currencies, daily rewards, purchases, or internet connection. Open the app and play.

THREE DISTINCT OPPONENTS
Luna applies selective pressure. Miles protects his stack. Rook embraces variance. Each opponent estimates unseen-card equity, considers the pot price, and adapts to how often you fold, call, and raise.

LEARN WHILE YOU PLAY
Toggle Odds at any point in a hand to see your estimated equity, current made hand, pot odds, and immediate drawing outs. Turn it off for a completely clean table.

REAL NO-LIMIT HOLD’EM
Play a four-handed tournament with rotating blinds, all-ins, side pots, eliminations, and complete showdown rules.

BUILT TO FEEL GOOD
Responsive card animation, subtle haptics, and original card, chip, fold, and win sounds make every action satisfying without slowing down the game.

Everything stays on your device. Your table is waiting.
