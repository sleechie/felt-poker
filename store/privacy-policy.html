Felt Poker

Privacy policy

Felt Poker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your current poker match and your sound and odds-panel preferences locally on your device. This information never leaves the device and is removed when you delete the app.

No accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking

Felt Poker has no user accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, tracking technologies, in-app purchases, or server connection. The app does not request access to contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, or other personal information.

Children’s privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version changes these practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the support link on Felt Poker’s App Store page.

Effective July 10, 2026 · Felt Poker 1.0
